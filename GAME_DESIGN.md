# Game Development Simulator - Design Document

## Core Vision

Create a deep, systemic, interactive Roblox simulator game where players build a game development studio from the ground up, within a dense, explorable, low-poly city designed specifically to support management gameplay, immersion, and long-term progression.

**This is not a clicker, idle, or menu-only game.**  
The world, buildings, and systems must work together to create a living ecosystem.

## World Generation Philosophy

### Primary Goal
Generate the best possible city for a game-development simulator:
- Small in footprint
- Massive in perceived depth
- Optimized for Roblox performance
- Purpose-built for gameplay systems

The world should feel handcrafted, even if procedurally assisted.

## City Scale & Illusion of Size

The city must be compact but visually dense. The player must never see the entire city from one viewpoint.

**Techniques:**
- Curved streets
- Elevation changes
- Sightline blockers
- Decorative buildings
- Tree clusters
- Alleyways

Every step should reveal new visual information.

## City Zoning System

### Central Hub Zone
- Player spawn plaza
- Navigation signage
- Close proximity to all major systems
- Feels like the heart of the city

### Commercial Zone
- Computer Store
- Interior Decor Store
- Hiring Agency
- Marketing Offices
- High foot traffic feel

### Studio Zone
- Player studio plots (12 exactly)
- Garage starter buildings
- Expansion buildings
- Clean, professional aesthetic

### Decorative Buffer Zone
- Non-enterable buildings
- Parks
- Trees
- Visual noise to increase scale

## Player Spawn & Flow Design

- Spawn at the center of the city
- Player should instinctively know where to go
- Roads and props subtly guide movement
- All essential locations reachable within 10–20 seconds

## Required Accessible Buildings

### Computer Store
- Sells hardware & upgrades
- Interior displays
- Physical shopping interactions

### Hiring Agency
- Hire employees
- NPC interactions
- Office interior

### Interior Decor Store
- Furniture
- Decorations
- Mood modifiers

### Garage Studio (Starter)
- No desks included
- Fits 2–4 desks
- Humble aesthetic

### Small Studio Building
- Fits 5–10 desks
- Early expansion

### Large Studio HQ
- Late-game building
- Massive interior

## Player Plots

- Exactly 12 empty plots
- Uniform size
- Near studio zone
- Visually separated
- Chosen at game start

## Owner Computer (Command Center)

Central management OS with:
- Remote purchases
- Analytics
- Research
- Hiring
- Strategic planning

## World Reactivity

- NPC movement
- Ambient animations
- Employee animations
- Dynamic lighting
- Day/night cycles (optional)

## City Design Quality Rules

- No empty space
- No dead ends without purpose
- Every building has visual intent
- Decorative buildings support scale illusion
- Interiors are clean, readable, optimized

## Performance Optimization

- Low-poly art style
- Optimized for Roblox physics & streaming
- Prefer fewer, higher-quality assets
- Avoid clutter overload
- Maintain consistent art style

## End Goal

Create a Roblox experience that feels like:
- SimCity meets Game Dev Tycoon
- But fully explorable
- Fully interactive
- Fully immersive

The player should feel:
- Ownership
- Progression
- Creative control
- Strategic depth
